# 📍 Google Maps Business Opportunity Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes local business data collected from Google Maps to identify businesses with strong customer ratings but limited online presence. The goal is to uncover opportunities where businesses can improve their digital visibility by establishing a website and strengthening their online presence.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Collect business data from Google Maps.
* Clean and preprocess the dataset.
* Analyze business ratings, reviews, and website availability.
* Identify high-potential businesses without websites.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate business insights and recommendations.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset includes information such as:

* Business Name
* Category
* Rating
* Review Count
* Website Availability
* Contact Number
* Address
* Latitude & Longitude

**Dataset Size:** 420+ Businesses

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Over **81%** of businesses do not have a website.
* Businesses without websites represent significant digital growth opportunities.
* Many highly rated businesses still rely only on Google Maps for their online presence.
* Categories such as retail, restaurants, and pharmacies show strong potential for digital transformation.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Encourage businesses to develop professional websites.
* Improve local SEO and Google Business Profiles.
* Leverage social media alongside a website to increase customer reach.
* Regularly collect customer reviews to strengthen online credibility.
