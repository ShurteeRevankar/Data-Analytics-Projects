# 🗺️ Google Maps Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Streamlit-based Business Analytics Dashboard** that allows users to upload any Google Maps business dataset, explore business insights, visualize trends, and predict business opportunities using Machine Learning.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Google Maps Business Analytics Dashboard is designed to help business owners, analysts, and researchers analyze Google Maps business listings. The application performs automatic data preprocessing, interactive visualizations, business analytics, and predictive analysis to identify potential business opportunities.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📂 Upload any Google Maps business dataset (CSV)
- 📊 Interactive KPI Dashboard
- 📈 Rating Analytics
- 🌍 Geographic Analysis
- 💻 Online Presence Analysis
- ⭐ Customer Reviews Analysis
- 🤖 Business Opportunity Prediction
- 🎯 Opportunity Finder
- 🔍 Business Search
- 📁 Data Explorer
- 📑 Model Explanation
